How this character counter works

Type or paste text and see the character count update as you write. Below the box, each row shows how close you are to a popular platform’s limit, and turns red when you go over it.

Limits shown

  • X (Twitter) post: 280 characters
  • Instagram caption: 2,200 characters, and bio: 150
  • LinkedIn post: 3,000 characters
  • YouTube title: 100, and description: 5,000
  • SEO title tag: about 60, and meta description: about 160

Platforms change their limits and count some things differently, such as links, emoji, or accented characters, so treat these numbers as a guide and check the platform before you publish. Search engines also cut titles and descriptions by pixel width, not characters, so the SEO numbers are approximate.

Frequently asked questions

Do spaces count as characters? Yes, in most platforms. The count without spaces is shown separately.

Is my text saved? No. Nothing leaves your browser, and nothing is stored.
