What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is filler text that designers and developers use to show how a layout looks before the real words are ready. Because it reads as nonsense, it keeps people focused on the design instead of the message.

How to use this generator

  • Pick paragraphs, sentences, or words and enter how many you need.
  • Leave the checkbox on if you want the classic opening line.
  • Click Generate, then Copy text and paste it into your design.
