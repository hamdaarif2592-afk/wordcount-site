What is a URL slug?

A slug is the readable part of a web address that names a page, like my-first-post in example.com/blog/my-first-post. Short, clean slugs are easier to read, share, and remember, and search engines use the words in them as a small hint about the page topic.

Tips for good slugs

  • Use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens. Hyphens are the usual choice for separating words.
  • Keep it short and describe the page with the main keywords.
  • Drop small words and special characters. Accents are converted to plain letters here.
  • Avoid changing a slug after the page is live, or add a redirect from the old address.
