How to use this word counter

Paste or type into the box above. The word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts update with every keystroke, so you can check a limit while you write. Use Copy text to take your draft with you, or Clear text to start fresh.

How the counts work

  • Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.
  • Characters include spaces and punctuation. The count without spaces is shown below the box.
  • Sentences are counted by ending punctuation: periods, question marks, and exclamation marks.
  • Reading time assumes about 238 words per minute. Speaking time assumes about 150 words per minute.

Common word limits

  • Meta description: about 155 characters
  • X (Twitter) post: 280 characters
  • College application essay: usually 250 to 650 words
  • Blog post that ranks well: often 1,000 to 2,000 words

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. All counting happens in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server. A draft is kept in your browser’s local storage so a refresh doesn’t lose it, and Clear text removes it.

Does it count hyphenated words as one word?

Yes. Words joined by a hyphen, like “well-known,” count as one word, which matches how most word processors count.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The page is built for small screens as well as desktops.
