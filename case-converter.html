What each case does

  • UPPERCASE and lowercase change every letter.
  • Title Case capitalizes main words and keeps short words like “a,” “of,” and “the” lowercase, except at the start.
  • Sentence case capitalizes the first letter of each sentence.
  • camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case turn phrases into names for code, files, and URLs.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent anywhere? No. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I undo a conversion? Press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) in the box right after converting, or paste your original text again.
