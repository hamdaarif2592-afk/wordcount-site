About this site

Word Counter (wordcount.win) is a small collection of free text tools that are fast, simple, and private. You paste or type your text, and the result appears right away, with no sign-up and no files to upload.

The tools

  • Word Counter: count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time.
  • Character Counter: check your text against X, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, SEO, and SMS limits.
  • Case Converter: switch between UPPERCASE, Title Case, camelCase, snake_case, and more.
  • Slug Generator: turn titles into clean URL slugs.
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator: create placeholder text.

Your text stays with you

Every tool runs in your browser. The text you type is not sent to our servers or stored by us. The privacy policy explains exactly what is and isn’t collected.

How the site is paid for

The tools are free to use. We may show ads to cover the costs of running the site, and we keep them out of the way of the tools themselves.
