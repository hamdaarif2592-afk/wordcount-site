Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

This policy explains what happens when you use wordcount.win (“the site”).

The text you enter

The tools run in your browser. Text you type or paste is not sent to our servers and we cannot see it. The Word Counter saves your draft in your browser’s local storage on your own device so a refresh doesn’t lose it. Clearing the text, or clearing your browser data, removes it.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate usage, such as page views, countries, and devices. It is designed to work without cookies or tracking individual visitors across sites.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your cookie banner choice in your browser’s local storage. If we show ads, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described below.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense or similar networks. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalized advertising in your Google Ad Settings or opt out at aboutads.info. Ads load only after you accept them in the banner where the law requires it.

Fonts

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google’s servers and Google can receive your IP address.

Your choices

You can decline cookies in the banner, clear your browser’s stored data at any time, or contact us to ask questions about this policy.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
